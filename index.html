M. Tuna Alatan

I am a senior undergraduate researcher at Bilkent University, working at the intersection of computer vision, generative modeling, and medical image reconstruction.

I study how generative models can solve inverse problems in computational imaging. My current work explores fast, physics-aware generative methods for accelerated multicoil MRI under the supervision of Prof. Tolga Çukur.
